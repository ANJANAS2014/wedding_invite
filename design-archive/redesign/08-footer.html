Audit: current section

  • Faded edge at the top. A 70px cream-to-burgundy gradient (Footer.css:7) creates a muddy band where the Wishes Wall ends.
  • Low-contrast sign-off. It's in gold-foil gradient text (.foil), about 3:1 contrast on burgundy at large size, and the gradient softens the letterforms.
  • Tiny date and excess padding. The date is 11px with .4em tracking (:22), and padding goes up to 110px top and 120px bottom (:4) for three lines of content.

System (from 01 · Our Events)

  • Same dark surface and header ornament as Events and RSVP. Script (Great Vibes) is used only for sign-offs, per --fs.

Design decisions

  • Solid gold-light script sign-off (no foil gradient), the shared ornament, and a 12px date label.
  • A clean hard edge against the Wishes Wall, with no fade band. Padding follows the system (64px / 40px) plus the iPhone home-indicator safe area at the bottom.
  • Kept as is: the final petal shower when the footer comes into view.
