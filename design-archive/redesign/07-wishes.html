Audit: current section

  • Header is crowded and the text too small. Eyebrow, title, subtitle, rule, an 11px uppercase count with italic hint, then 11px tracked tab labels. The tabs are text with an underline, not clear 44px buttons.
  • Hazy pond. It has horizontal scanlines across the whole panel, a blurred glowing moon and a soft light column, so the "night" reads as low-resolution.
  • Oversized pond. It's about 560px tall on desktop for a few lamps, and the reading card overlaps its lower edge. Prev / Read full wish / Next are 11px tracked links.
  • Grain over everything. The .grain texture covers the whole section, including the postcard text.
  • "Write your own wish" is a small underlined link. It's 11px tracked uppercase, not a clear button.

System (from 01 · Our Events)

  • Same header, type scale, spacing, 44px controls and light surface as Bride & Groom and Our Story.
  • The segmented pill toggle introduced here is the standard pattern for any two-view switch.

Design decisions

  • The view switch is a 44px segmented pill: "✦ Floating lamps" / "☰ Read all wishes". The count and hint sit on one 16px line.
  • The pond keeps the idea (night water, moon, floating diyas) but is drawn crisply: a solid gradient sky, a sharp moon, a thin light path and vector diyas. It's 380px tall instead of about 560px. The lit lamp has a gold ring. The reading card sits below the lamps (never over them) as a solid ivory card: 22px text clamped to 3 lines, then three 44px buttons.
  • "Read all wishes" is an even grid of postcards: message, name, and a 44px ♥ like button. It's shown below as a second state, and on the live site the toggle switches between them.
  • Kept as is: lamp auto-cycling, new-wish arrival animation, the full-wish modal, photos on postcards and the auto-scrolling rail when there are many wishes.
