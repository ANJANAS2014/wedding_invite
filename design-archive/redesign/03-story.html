Audit: current section

  • Narrow and tall on desktop. A single 380px card in the middle of a 1440px screen leaves about 70% of the width empty. The section is 1232px tall, with padding up to 90px (OurStoryAndFilmstripSection.css:12) and a 72px gap above "Read the full story" (:121).
  • Caption printed over the photo. Title and text sit on a gradient over the image (.story-card figcaption), so contrast depends on the photo. On the first photo the text sits on light clothing and a geometric print.
  • Text too small. The "1 / 6" counter is 10px with wide tracking (:476) and the eyebrow is 11px (:22). The "Tap to continue" hint is 15px grey italic.
  • Navigation is hidden. Next and previous are invisible tap zones on each half of the photo, so on desktop nothing tells you the card is clickable or how many moments there are.
  • Two story layouts in one file. The old editorial "chapters" styles (.st-ch, .st-ph, .st-tx, :32–150) are still in the CSS alongside the story cards. Only the cards are used.

System (from 01 · Our Events)

  • Same wrap, spacing, type scale, header and 44px buttons as 01 and 02. Light surface, same as Bride & Groom.
  • Caption rule: text never sits on a photo. It goes in a solid burgundy panel attached to the image, which comfortably clears 4.5:1 contrast.

Design decisions

  • Desktop: a balanced two-column layout. The story card is on the left, and on the right are the hook line, an index of the six moments and the "Read the full story" button. The active moment is marked with a gold rule and shows its text. Clicking a moment jumps to it.
  • Mobile: hook, then the card with its caption in a solid panel below the photo, then the hint and button. Previous/next arrow buttons (44px) can be shown on desktop hover. In the mockup they're left out to keep the photo clean.
  • Kept as is: auto-advance (5.5s), the progress bars, swipe, tap and hold-to-pause, and the expanding full story text (styled as 16px body paragraphs, max 60ch).
  • Height: desktop 831px (was 1232, 33% shorter). Mobile 1009px (was 1001): the caption now sits below the photo instead of over it and is always readable, at almost no extra height.
