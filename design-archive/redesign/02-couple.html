Audit: current section

  • Very tall. The section is 1649px on desktop. Padding goes up to 96px (CoupleSection.css:9), with up to 56px gaps above the portraits (:35) and 40–50px above the game and the vows (:422, :521, :691).
  • Labels too small. Many labels are 9–10px uppercase (CoupleSection.css:148, 165, 376, 414, 451, 501, 528, 594, 678, 702), including the "Tap a photo" hint.
  • Game cards waste space. Each card is about 175px tall for a one-line question, and the Lalith/Anjana buttons are under 44px. On mobile the carousel cuts the next card with no sign there are more.
  • Narrow story columns on mobile. At 390px each portrait's story is squeezed into about 160px, so it runs to 4–5 centred lines.
  • Unbalanced pairing on desktop. The portraits sit far apart, the "&" and thali float high, and the chain droops below the photo line. The two vows break onto different line counts.
  • Soft effects. A grain texture over the section (.grain) and blur-up image reveals (.bu) soften the photos.

System (from 01 · Our Events)

  • Same 1120px wrap, spacing scale, type scale, header (kicker / title / ornament) and 44px buttons as 01.
  • Light-surface version of the system: ivory background, burgundy titles, antique-gold labels and hairlines at 22–35% opacity. Same rules as the dark Events section.
  • Proposes one new token, --leaf (muted botanical green), for later sections. It isn't used here.

Design decisions

  • The "The Groom" / "The Bride" labels are dropped, because the heading and the Team pills already say it. That's the same call as Keralam/Chennai in Events.
  • Portraits, "&" and thali sit on one centred axis, at 216px on desktop and 128px on mobile. The heritage rings stay: Kanjeevaram temple border for Lalith, kasavu for Anjana. The "Team" pill sits on the ring's lower edge, the same tab pattern as "Next celebration" in Events.
  • The stories sit below the pair. On desktop they're two equal centred columns; on mobile they're two full-width paragraphs led by the name, which removes the narrow columns.
  • Game: a compact 3×2 grid on desktop, and a snap carousel with dots on mobile. Card 01 is shown answered, to document the back of the card. All buttons are 44px.
  • Height: desktop is 6% shorter. Mobile grows by about 200px: the old section used 9–15px text and 30px buttons, which the system no longer allows. The answered game card shown here is also the tallest card state.
  • Vows: two equal columns split by a hairline, with balanced line breaks. They stack on mobile.
  • Kept from the current site (shown static here): the tug-of-war tally and the "You're on Team…" note appear once a side is picked, along with the toast, the pinned badge and the chain draw-in animation.
