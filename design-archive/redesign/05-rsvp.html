Audit: current section

  • The name field is barely visible. Step 1 is just an underline with a floating label that only appears on focus, so the first thing guests must fill in looks empty.
  • Send button looks disabled. It's muted brown with 13px text tracked wide (RsvpSection.css:902–906), and the hint above it is 13px.
  • Text and taps too small. 10–11px labels (:678, :855, :870, :1020, :1175), an 11px character counter (:294), a 32px Add Emoji button (:161, :558), 36px steppers (:264) and a 40px photo button (:862).
  • Controls scattered around the wish box. "Add photo" floats top-right above it while "Add Emoji" and the counter sit below.
  • Two style generations stacked. The CSS is 1247 lines, and a full #s5.rs override set starts at :576. It's fragile to change.
  • Loose spacing. Padding goes up to 90px (:578), and the steps on desktop leave wide gaps.

System (from 01 · Our Events)

  • Same header, spacing, type scale and 44px controls. Dark burgundy section, the same as Events.
  • New pattern, the reply card: an ivory card (--card) with a gold hairline and a soft second ring, used here for the form. It gives ink-on-ivory contrast for inputs.

Design decisions

  • The form becomes an ivory reply card inside the burgundy section, like the reply card in an invitation envelope. Inputs are white with a gold border, 48px tall, with 18px text and always-visible labels.
  • Each step has one header line: a numbered circle, a 20px title and 16px helper text. The steps are separated by hairlines, with no extra boxes.
  • The two celebrations are equal side-by-side panels on desktop, each with a Yes/Sorry segmented control. The wedding panel is shown answered "Yes" so the 44px guest stepper is visible. They stack on mobile.
  • Emoji, Photo and the counter sit in one row under the wish box. "Send my RSVP ❤️" is a solid burgundy 52px button. Until both celebrations are answered, the hint above it explains why, but the button no longer looks broken.
  • Kept as is: the wax-seal envelope animation and the confirmation panel (they would sit on this card), the "we've saved a seat for you" line for personal links, the error messages and the photo preview.
