Audit: current section

  • Portrait photos badly cropped. Every frame is forced to 3:2 landscape (OurStoryAndFilmstripSection.css:274), so the five portrait photos lose about half their height, sometimes cutting through faces.
  • Unreadable edge codes. They are sized at var(--rail) * .24 (:322), which is about 9px on desktop and 6px on mobile.
  • Pause control is detached and small. It floats alone in the top-right corner, far from the strip (:192–213), as 11px tracked text in a 40px-tall button.
  • Hazy glow. A large radial "light-table" glow sits behind the strip (:177–187), adding haze around otherwise crisp frames.
  • Big loose gaps. The section is 530px tall on desktop for a 300px strip, with padding up to 36px and 40px plus the teaser's own margin.

System (from 01 · Our Events)

  • Same spacing, type and 44px button rules. Light surface, continuing Our Story.
  • Minimum 11px text applies to the decorative film edge codes too.

Design decisions

  • Every photo is shown whole. Each frame keeps its photo's orientation: 3:2 for landscape, 4:5 for portrait, all at one height (224px desktop, 168px mobile). Nothing is cropped through a face. Real film frames are all landscape; this trades that detail for honest photos.
  • The rails keep the perforations and SCRL edge codes, now 11px IBM Plex Mono. The light-table glow is removed and the strip has one subtle shadow.
  • Pause/Play is a 44px button centred under the strip, where your eye already is. It still pauses on hover, and while a finger is held down on mobile.
  • The strip continues straight on from Our Story, so its top padding is 24px instead of a full section gap. Height: 508px on desktop (was 530). Mobile is 420px (was 377), because the rails carry 11px codes and the Pause button is 44px tall.
  • Kept as is: the 95px/s scroll, seamless loop, tap to open the lightbox, and frame numbers. The mockup is static.
