What's new in the premium shower
  • Real depth: three layers. Tiny soft petals far away, crisp ones in the middle, and an occasional large out-of-focus petal close to the screen (bokeh).
  • Petals that tumble in 3D: they flip and show a darker back. They fall faster edge-on and float when flat, like real petals.
  • Lit petals: each one has a deeper colour at the base, a lighter edge, soft veins and a sheen.
  • Gold leaf flakes that catch the light as they turn.
  • Wind: a gentle breeze with occasional gusts, instead of a fixed side-to-side sway.
  • Reacts to the guest: scrolling adds parallax, and a finger or cursor brushes petals aside.
  • Moments: a welcome shower on open, and a burst from the “Send blessings” button.
  • Fewer petals (14–30, depending on screen size). It looks calmer and more refined.
